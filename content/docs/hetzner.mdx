
## How to activate the promo code

1. Sign up for free at [hetzner.com](https://www.hetzner.com/) and create your Hetzner account.
2. Go to **User Account → Promo Codes** in the menu.
3. Enter your code and click **Redeem**—that’s it.
4. The credit will be automatically applied to your next invoices.
